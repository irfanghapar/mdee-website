The Man Behind
the Sauce

M’Dee began in 2006, when Mohd Rosidi Abdul Rashid — then a computer science lecturer in Bukit Mertajam — bottled his char koay teow paste five hundred bottles at a time. He left teaching to run it full time. Today the kitchen in Tasek Gelugor turns out thousands of bottles a month for kitchens all over Malaysia.

The secret to real Penang char koay teow, in one bottle.

Our 1.5kg Sos Char Kuey Teow is the one the stalls reach for — more than 20,000 bottles sold online. Add your own prawns, egg and kuey teow, and that’s it.
